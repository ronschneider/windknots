Any tips on how to set up my indicator rig? Running an 8 wgt Spey rod and im going 10ft of 20lb Mono down to a swivel with 12 lb mono. I also sometimes run a 0x leader and blood knot to a 1x tippet. From the knot/swivel: It’s about 8in to my first heavy fly or bead, then another 8 from the bend of the hook down to my next fly.
